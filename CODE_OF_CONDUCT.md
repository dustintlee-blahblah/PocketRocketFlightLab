# Code of conduct

Pocket Rocket Flight Lab follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful to everyone, whatever their background or experience; disagree about ideas, not
people; give and accept constructive feedback; and look after newcomers, many of whom are new to rocketry, to code or
to both. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing other people's private information aren't
acceptable, here or in other project spaces such as issues, pull requests and discussions.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, email the maintainer, Dustin Lee, at
[dustintlee@gmail.com](mailto:dustintlee@gmail.com). Reports are kept private, and you'll get a reply. The maintainer
may remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, or temporarily or
permanently ban, anyone who does, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
